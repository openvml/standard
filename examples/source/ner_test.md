### Character Alias Reference

* **Mira** — the little singer, college student, blonde girl, fair-haired girl, the
  young performer.
* **Clara** — the girl with glasses, top student, college scholar, the nerd, the
  bespectacled student.
* **Bran** — the old boatswain, sea dog, veteran sailor, old deckhand, tattooed bruiser.
* **Gareth** — the mustached cook, chef, kitchen master, the cook, owner of the galley.
* **Victor** — the grim bouncer, big guy, heavyset guard, enforcer, the man keeping order.
* **Selene** — the demanding diva, model, brunette, tall fashionista, glamorous woman.

---

### Opening Scene

In the dim back room of a harbor tavern called **The Rusty Anchor**, an unlikely group had gathered
around a corner table.

**Mira** nervously plucked at the strings of her silent guitar, feeling the jealous stare of
**Clara** behind her. The bespectacled student methodically cleaned her glasses while checking
something in a battered notebook.

A little farther away, **Bran** sat lazily sipping cheap rum. His tattooed fingers tapped out an
irregular rhythm against the wooden table.

From the kitchen came the clatter of pots and pans. **Gareth** was arguing with a supplier again,
his booming voice carrying through the entire tavern.

At the bar, **Victor** looked bored, tossing a shiny coin into the air and catching it without even
looking.

A few minutes later, **Selene** swept into the room with the confidence of someone who expected
everyone to stop and watch her. Her bright makeup contrasted sharply with her strict business suit.

### Dialogue

   — If we don't start the show in five minutes, the audience is going to tear this place
   apart, —
   squeaked **the little singer**, pushing a loose strand of blonde hair back into her ponytail.

   — Calm down, — replied **the girl with glasses** dryly without looking up from her notes. —
   According to my calculations, the crowd won't reach its peak for another thirty minutes.

   **The old boatswain** gave a low chuckle through his mustache and finished his rum. — Your
   calculations forgot that three cargo ships were unloaded at the harbor today. There's
   nowhere left
   to stand.

   — And who's going to pay for all the broken dishes? — shouted **the mustached cook** from
   behind the
   kitchen curtain, waving a wooden ladle threateningly.

   A lazy voice came from the corner of the bar. — Relax, chef. My people are controlling the
   entrance.
   We won't let anyone extra inside, — said **the grim bouncer**, catching the falling coin in his
   palm.

   At that moment the doors swung open, and **the demanding diva** entered the room with a
   theatrical
   sigh. — What a dump. You can practically smell the cheap beer and stale ale.

   — Oh, look who's finally here, — sneered **the singer**, deliberately turning her back
   toward the
   wall. — We've only been waiting for you for an hour, Your Highness.

   — Traffic, darling, — said **the model**, waving dismissively while inspecting her flawless
   manicure. — Besides, my time is worth more than this little concert of yours.

   **The top student** finally closed her notebook and gave the newcomer a stern look. —
   We're supposed
   to work as a team. Your ego is destroying the entire schedule.

    — We've heard your lectures before, — snorted **the brunette**, walking toward the
    mirror
    beside the
    stage. — Where's our sponsor?

    — Our distinguished investor, — answered **the sea dog**, — is currently calculating the
    losses from
    the last storm. So we'll have to manage on our own.

    — Wonderful, — muttered **the chef**, returning to the stove. — That means I'll probably
    miss
    another bonus for the banquet.

    — Stop whining, — growled **the tattooed bruiser**, getting up from his chair. — Hey,
    bartender,
    pour me another one before these strings snap.

    **The big guy** behind the counter silently filled a glass and pushed it toward the
    musician. —
    That's the last one on the house. After that, you pay full price.

    — Hey, **the college student**, — called **the tall fashionista** from the stage, — hand
    me that
    microphone. And be careful. Don't dirty the cable.

    **The fair-haired girl** flushed with indignation. — What, did your hands fall off? Get
    it yourself.
    I'm not your servant!

    — Ladies, break it up, — **the man keeping order** said lazily. — If you start a fight,
    I'll throw
    both of you out without severance pay.

    — Fine, — sighed **the bespectacled student**. — Guitarist ready. Vocals ready. We start
    on the
    first signal.

    From the kitchen came the final chord of **the kitchen master's** culinary symphony. — Good
    riddance! Just stay away from my serving counter!

    — Well then, here we go, — grinned **the old sailor**, taking one final sip and winking
    at **the
    fair-haired girl**.
